S79 - New World Cup format. Qualifying is played in the odd season before the World Cup: the top 15 countries by rating qualify automatically (the host replaces #15 if it is not in the top 15, and #15 then plays qualifying); the other 70 play 6 single games against 6 different random opponents and the top 49 advance. The World Cup has 64 teams in 16 groups of 4 (seeded pots, host in group A, each team plays each group rival twice); the top 2 of each group advance to a 32-team single-elimination bracket. Tiebreakers: wins, head-to-head, point differential, random. Country rosters are the best player at every position among the FBAD2 teams located in that country, redecided when the World Cup phase starts.
